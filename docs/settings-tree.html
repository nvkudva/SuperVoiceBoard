WaveKey settings — proposed grouping

All 243 menu entries at every level. Red pills say where a row sits today. Drag any row to move it — drop on the top or bottom edge of another row to place it beside that row, or on the middle to drop it inside. Arrow buttons and ↑ ↓ ← → still work. Double-click a label (or hit ✎) to rename it — the old wording is kept and listed below the outline. The outline on the right follows along — copy it back to me when the order is right.
