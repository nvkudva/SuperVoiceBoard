AI fix — four alternatives

Each shown the way it actually appears: in the toolbar tile at 52dp idle, then lit (violet ground, dark glyph) the way it renders while a fix is running, then at 36dp and 72dp. All four are original artwork, so no new licence enters the repo.

Sparkle

ic_ai_fix_sparkle

The mark everything else uses for AI. Nothing about text, so it leans on the strip's context — but it is unmistakable at 24dp and the only one that stays legible when the key lights up.

Text + sparkle

ic_ai_fix_text_sparkle

Says what the key acts on. Closest to the current icon, minus the wand: the lines carry the meaning and the sparkle says who did it.

Wand

ic_ai_fix_wand

Apply-something-to-this. The most active of the four, and the least specific — a wand could be any transform, not a rewrite.

Check + sparkle

ic_ai_fix_check_sparkle

The result rather than the act — the text came back fixed. Reads calmest of the four; risks looking like the autocorrect toggle, which is already a check.

Current — grammar wand

ic_ai_fix_current

Fluent's text-grammar-wand. Six shapes inside 24dp: three text lines, a wand and two sparkles. It is the busiest thing on the strip and the lines disappear first when the key is small or lit.
